# Notices review (#346's design, master at c1f5d2e)

Every notice Phantom can tell, drawn from the real constructors (`client::display` wording, `Notice`, `model::…`) in the GUI at 960×600 with the list open, light and dark. The action notices (Removed, Deleted, Renamed) went through the real flows against the in-memory service; the others were told with the same constructors the app calls (the scene is a review harness, not merged). Each still is cropped to the popover and the bar; the pager, connection and mid-undo ones to the bar.

**Legend.** Kind: R receipt (✓, brief), A action with Undo (✓, 1 min), W warning (⚠, until dismissed), E error (⛔, until dismissed). Line = the bar's words; list = what the popover adds.

**Result: 48 notices drawn, 36 with a problem; 7 connection states (five words, Restart beside Undo, the gap during an undo), 4 with a problem.** Worst five first, then the tables.

## The five worst

1. **Add of a magnet that fails** ([light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/add-1-light.png) · [dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/add-1-dark.png) · [640×320](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/min-add-1-min-dark.png)): the whole magnet link is the "name", one unbreakable string, and it is **clipped at the card's right edge** (`…&dn=No`). The reason sits under a black wall of hash.
2. **Couldn't undo the Delete, with stranded paths** ([light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/cant-undo-stranded-light.png) · [dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/cant-undo-stranded-dark.png) · [640×320](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/min-cant-undo-stranded-min-light.png)): two names, then one five-line run-on sentence with two full paths inside it. Nothing to scan; at 640×320 it covers the list.
3. **Part of a file in the Trash not saved** ([light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/part-light.png) · [dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/part-dark.png)): four lines of prose, the file path inside the sentence, and a second sentence explaining what a restart would do. No button, no structure.
4. **Settings reset** ([light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/reset-many-light.png) · [dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/reset-many-dark.png) · [640×320](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/min-reset-many-min-light.png)): bare labels ("UPnP", "App icon") with nothing saying they were reset, then a long paragraph ("They couldn't be read, so they're back to their defaults. The file as it was is kept as settings.invalid.json in the data dir.") explaining what the two buttons already do.
5. **"Delete Evening undone"** ([light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/preset-undone-light.png) · [dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HugeLetters/phantom/pr-artifacts/notices-review/preset-undone-dark.png)): a Look Preset's name, which the user chose, is in the **bar's line**, against the rule; and there is no list to move it to.

Also: the mid-undo gap and the Restart + Undo crowding, under *Connection and states*.

## Patterns behind the problems

1. **Paths and OS errors inside sentences** (24, 26–29, 31, 40–42): the list has a name/reason split for batches, but single notices put a path, a reason and an OS error in one paragraph. Give every notice the same three parts: names, the reason on one line, paths each on a line.
2. **Notes that explain** (16, 17, 20–22, 24, 27): "Its files stay where they are", "so it won't be downloaded again", "The file as it was is kept as … in the data dir". Rule: what happens, not why.
3. **Severity and wording of the same failure differ** (33 vs 45; "Couldn't undo the X" vs "Couldn't be undone"; "Not moved:" and "Couldn't move").
4. **User names or file names in a bar line** (15, 47).
5. **Connection words are muted** while their dot and the buttons carry the state (C4–C6).

## Not drawn headless, and how things were drawn

- A **Move to ▸ failure from the clash dialog** ("Couldn't move the torrent" after a refused folder) and a **Look-Preset-name reset** ("Tone in Dark of a saved Look Preset" line, preset name in the list) are told by the same code as 39 and 24 with other text; not drawn separately.
- **Rename refused in the header field** and other what's-known-before-anything-is-sent cases are tooltips, not notices (#347).
- A **real Finder error from macOS** can't happen headless; 36–38 use the app's own `Finder` seam with a stub.
- A **real librqbit add error** (34, 35) uses text typed in by the harness; the card is the real one, the wording of a live error may differ.
- Notices 17, 19, 21, 22, 23, 27–29, 33–34, 39–42, 44–48 were told by the harness with the app's own constructors, not by their real triggers (no multi-select, no real disk failures headless). The action notices 16, 18, 20 went through the real flows.
